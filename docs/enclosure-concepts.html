Enclosure concepts

Four directions for the same internals

Same Pi, e-ink panel, speaker, and light module underneath — four different physical treatments. These are illustrated concept sketches, not renders; pick a direction or take pieces from each.

Pebble

One soft-edged block sized to fit in a hand. The light comes from a channel along the top edge instead of a separate fixture, so the screen and speaker share a single uninterrupted face.

matte plastic · ~110×70×45mm · encoder on the right edge

Dial

A small wooden cabinet with a real dial. The clip-light becomes a literal hood over the screen — the same idea as the old Kindle case, just built in instead of clipped on.

walnut + brushed aluminum · ~140×60×70mm · one front knob

Column

Stands instead of lying flat, so it claims almost no nightstand space. Light, screen, and speaker stack vertically on a small turned base.

ceramic or printed cylinder · ~50×50×150mm · twist-cap encoder

Rig

Doesn't hide the build. Frame, driver, and mounting hardware stay visible on purpose, screen tilted slightly like it's been set down rather than built in.

acrylic standoffs + exposed driver · footprint varies · side-mounted controls
